Tips for the right fit

Measure your feet in the evening, when they are at their largest, from the back of the heel to the tip of the longest toe.

If you are between sizes, consider the width of your foot and the style of shoe: loafers usually fit closer than lace-ups.

Sizing varies between brands and lasts, so treat this chart as a guide and check the brand's own size guide before ordering. For Whimsical Walk shoes, see our size guide.
